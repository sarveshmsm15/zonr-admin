Cash payments, weekly settlement and records

Cash bookings: when available, choosing Pay by cash authorizes the confirmed booking QR, host contact and entrance directions without representing a bank-verified online payment. The customer pays the entire displayed total, including service cost, directly to the host on arrival. The host scans the booking QR and records full cash collection. The service cost enters the host weekly ledger, due the following Monday (IST). Hosts transfer the exact reported service-cost amount to ZONR and submit a 12-digit bank reference. An administrator independently verifies receipt before settling only the bookings covered by that report. Reporting alone never clears a balance; later dues and financial history remain. Measured EV energy is a separate bill. Before cash is collected, cancellation creates no cash refund or service-cost debt; after arrival or collection contact support for any cancellation, refund, incident or statutory-remedy review. ZONR does not debit the host bank account automatically.

Approved booking date and time changes

For an eligible future booking after payment or cash selection, the customer may request another arrival date/time for the same duration and agreed price. The original reservation stays active while the request is pending. Only the authorized host may approve or deny it. Approval checks current opening hours, space capacity and reserved-charger availability, updates the booked times and replaces the entry pass. Denial leaves the original slot unchanged. Changes after arrival, during a charging session or after the original start require support.
