More about payment, availability and privacy

Parking Space: A designated place to park your vehicle. EV Charging: Charging for compatible electric vehicles. Booking: A request for a selected parking time. Check-in: Host scan confirmation of your booking QR. Check-out: The end of your parking or charging session.

Bank review: Your payment report is checked against official bank records before verification. The countdown does not approve a payment automatically.

Availability: An estimate rechecked before booking, not a sensor guarantee.

Find My Vehicle: Private bay notes and optional approximate GPS after a host-confirmed scanned arrival. GPS does not provide indoor positioning.

Platform fee: A separate disclosed fee shown before payment. GST is not currently collected because supplier registration is not active.

App previews: Illustrative screens and artwork. Actual spaces, facilities, prices and availability vary.

How ZONR works · payments and time changes

Choose a parking space and time, send your request, and wait for the host to accept. Pay the exact displayed total by UPI and request bank review, or choose Pay by cash when available. Cash customers pay the full total, including service cost, to the host on arrival. The host scans the booking QR and records collection; the service cost enters a weekly balance due the following Monday (IST). Hosts report their transfer with the bank reference. ZONR verifies the bank receipt before settling only covered bookings; newer dues and history remain.

For eligible future bookings after payment or cash selection, request a different arrival date/time for the same duration and agreed price. Your original slot stays reserved until the host approves after checking hours, space and charger availability. Denial preserves the original reservation. After arrival or collection, contact support for changes or refunds. Measured EV energy is billed separately.
