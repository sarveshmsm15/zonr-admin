Cancellation policy

Wildarion Technologies · Effective 29 October 2026 · India · 1.0.0

Cash payments, weekly settlement and records

Cash bookings: when available, choosing Pay by cash authorizes the confirmed booking QR, host contact and entrance directions without representing a bank-verified online payment. The customer pays the entire displayed total, including service cost, directly to the host on arrival. The host scans the booking QR and records full cash collection. The service cost enters the host weekly ledger, due the following Monday (IST). Hosts transfer the exact reported service-cost amount to ZONR and submit a 12-digit bank reference. An administrator independently verifies receipt before settling only the bookings covered by that report. Reporting alone never clears a balance; later dues and financial history remain. Measured EV energy is a separate bill. Before cash is collected, cancellation creates no cash refund or service-cost debt; after arrival or collection contact support for any cancellation, refund, incident or statutory-remedy review. ZONR does not debit the host bank account automatically.

Approved booking date and time changes

For an eligible future booking after payment or cash selection, the customer may request another arrival date/time for the same duration and agreed price. The original reservation stays active while the request is pending. Only the authorized host may approve or deny it. Approval checks current opening hours, space capacity and reserved-charger availability, updates the booked times and replaces the entry pass. Denial leaves the original slot unchanged. Changes after arrival, during a charging session or after the original start require support.

Incidents, service failure and legal remedies

An owner pausing a listing does not automatically cancel confirmed bookings. Report an unsafe or unusable space, host non-attendance, charging failure or disputed vehicle/property incident promptly with your booking reference. Customer voluntary-cancellation charges are not automatically applied to host or platform service failure. Refunds and any compensation depend on the facts, original booking terms, verified payments and applicable law. Host/customer responsibility for premises, vehicle custody or belongings does not remove ZONR's own duties or mandatory consumer remedies. We may facilitate complaints; consumers retain access to competent consumer commissions, regulators and courts.

Device access, availability and download counts

First launch offers system-controlled location, notification, microphone and camera permission setup. The device owner can refuse or revoke access. Photos can be captured with the camera or selected individually. Nearby-first ordering uses the selected area or available location; filters override the browsing scope and availability is checked before booking. Server or provider failures can occur even with internet access. Authorized admins see registered-member totals and APK download-request counts. Anonymous download clicks do not reveal a visitor identity and are not verified installations. Signed-in requests can be associated with that account. Host contact attempts and availability changes are recorded for support. An admin can make an unavailable listing inactive for new bookings; existing bookings remain accessible. The host may request reactivation, which requires team review. Existing cancellation rights remain unchanged.

GST and price transparency

GST is not currently collected. Cancellation and eligible refunds use the original verified payment and the booking policy; changing draft tax settings does not add tax to old bookings or deduct it again on refund. Tax invoice and credit-note operations must be configured and reviewed before any future GST collection. The displayed cancellation refund and statutory exceptions remain subject to the applicable policy and law.

Discounted requests

Cancellation or host denial does not automatically restore a consumed coupon use. Where a payment review or refund is applicable, support considers the amount actually paid, including the applied discount, rather than the original undiscounted parking quote. A coupon is not cash and cannot itself be refunded.

Before the start time

Open Bookings and select Cancel on your own pending or confirmed booking before its start. The server checks ownership and time; a successful cancellation releases the reserved capacity. Refresh the booking to confirm its new state. Repeating a completed decision does not create a new cancellation.

Host acceptance or denial

A host may accept or deny an eligible pending request before it starts. A denial releases capacity. Already confirmed, denied, cancelled or expired requests cannot be accepted again. If the host cannot honor a confirmed booking, contact support with its booking number; there is no separate automatic host-cancellation/refund button.

After the start or for access problems

Self-service cancellation is unavailable after the start. Use Help and support or the ZONR chat bot to create a ticket, include the booking number and describe the access or service issue. Support will review the booking and applicable rights. Removing a host listing from public search does not automatically cancel bookings already made.

Payments and refund review

Cancelling a booking, denying a request or opening a ticket does not automatically reverse a UPI transfer. If debited for a cancelled or denied booking, unable to access the space, or charged twice, request a payment/refund review with the booking and bank reference. Do not send a UPI PIN, OTP or bank password. Support must check official transaction records and arrange any refund due separately; the app does not currently issue automatic refunds.

Amounts, timing and charging disputes

For new bookings under the 25 October 2026 policy, customer cancellation before the booked start after payment is verified returns 50% of the amount actually paid for parking and its platform fee, rounded up to the nearest paise. The remaining 50% is retained as the cancellation charge. Existing bookings retain their agreed policy. Refunds are reviewed and transferred manually; cancellation does not itself move money. Host or platform cancellation, service failure, duplicate debits and statutory remedies are reviewed separately and may require a full refund or other remedy. Statutory rights prevail. Separate EV energy already supplied is handled separately. Bank processing times vary; contact support if an approved refund is delayed. Do not pay again while a debit is being investigated.
